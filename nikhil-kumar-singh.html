Profile

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Chemical Engineering, IIT Delhi, working with Prof. Abhijeet Raj on carbon capture and its downstream utilization. My experimental work concerns amine-based absorption–desorption processes for CO2 capture, focusing on solvent stability: thermal and oxidative degradation of amines, degradation kinetics, and the behaviour of blended amine formulations.

In parallel, I develop an integrated process concept that recovers carbon-free hydrogen from waste H2S through sulfur-recovery-coupled thermal cracking and routes it, together with captured CO2, into methanol synthesis. This strand combines Aspen Plus and Aspen HYSYS flowsheet modelling, reaction kinetics, MATLAB-based multi-objective optimization, and techno-economic assessment of capital and operating costs, with the aim of designing sulfur- and carbon-closed industrial processes.

Research Themes

Amine-based CO2 capture and solvent stability

  • Absorption–desorption process design and operation for post-combustion CO2 capture
  • Thermal and oxidative degradation of amine solvents under process conditions
  • Degradation kinetics of single and blended amine formulations
  • Linking solvent stability to regeneration energy and operating cost

Sulfur recovery, hydrogen from H2S, and CO2 utilization

  • Claus / sulfur recovery unit modelling and thermal cracking of H2S to H2 and elemental sulfur
  • Integration of recovered carbon-free hydrogen with captured CO2 for methanol synthesis
  • Kinetic modelling of the methanol converter and of the acid-gas reaction network
  • Multi-objective process optimization and techno-economic analysis (CAPEX / OPEX)
