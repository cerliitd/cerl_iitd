About Me

I am an M.Tech. student in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. My research focuses on reaction engineering and chemical process simulation, with a particular emphasis on kinetic modelling and process development for sustainable chemical and energy systems.

Current Research

My current work involves the kinetic-based modeling and process simulation of methanol synthesis, coupled with H2S thermal cracking for hydrogen production and sulfur recovery. This research includes reactor modeling, reaction kinetics, process integration, and process optimization using Aspen Plus.

Research Interests
  • Reaction Kinetics & Reactor Modeling
  • Methanol Synthesis
  • Hydrogen Production
  • Sulfur Recovery
  • Chemical Process Simulation
  • Process Integration & Optimization
  • Aspen Plus
