Biography

Abhijeet Raj is a Professor in the Department of Chemical Engineering at IIT Delhi, which he joined as Associate Professor in 2022 and where he has been Professor since July 2025. He took his PhD at the University of Cambridge in 2010 and his B.Tech. at IIT Guwahati in 2006. Before returning to India he spent nine years in the UAE, as Assistant Professor at The Petroleum Institute and then Associate Professor at Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi, and three years at KAUST in Saudi Arabia as a postdoctoral fellow and research scientist.

His research spans combustion kinetics, soot and pollutant formation, acid gas processing and hydrogen production. He is an Editor of the Journal of Environmental Chemical Engineering (Elsevier) and is listed among the top 2% of scientists worldwide by citation impact in the Stanford University ranking.

Research Focus: Reaction Engineering, Chemical Process Simulation, Process Integration

About Me: I am an M.Tech. student in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. My research focuses on reaction engineering and chemical process simulation, with a particular emphasis on kinetic modelling and process development for sustainable chemical and energy systems.

Current Research: My current work involves the kinetic-based modeling and process simulation of methanol synthesis, coupled with H2S thermal cracking for hydrogen production and sulfur recovery. This research includes reactor modeling, reaction kinetics, process integration, and process optimization using Aspen Plus.

Research Interests: Reaction Kinetics & Reactor Modeling; Methanol Synthesis; Hydrogen Production; Sulfur Recovery; Chemical Process Simulation; Process Integration & Optimization; Aspen Plus
