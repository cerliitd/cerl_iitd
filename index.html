Combustion Engineering Research Laboratory

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

CERL investigates the fundamental and applied aspects of combustion engineering, working toward a deeper understanding of reactive flow, fuel chemistry, and energy systems. The laboratory is led by Prof. Abhijeet Raj in the Department of Chemical Engineering at IIT Delhi.

About

About CERL

The Combustion Engineering Research Laboratory (CERL) is based in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. The laboratory is led by Prof. Abhijeet Raj and brings together postdoctoral researchers and doctoral scholars working on problems in combustion engineering.
